Selection Process

1
Submit Application Fill out the demo request form with your preferred day and time.
2
WhatsApp Confirmation Our academic team confirms your time slot and sends the Google Meet link.
3
Live Google Meet Demo Demonstrate a short 10–15 min teaching sample with our academic panel.
4
Onboarding & Student Allocation Selected teachers receive orientation and student batch allocations.

Evaluation Criteria

Our academic panel assesses:

  • Quran Tajweed accuracy & Makharij
  • Patience & warm communication with young learners
  • Online whiteboard & digital teaching readiness
  • Punctuality and professional commitment
